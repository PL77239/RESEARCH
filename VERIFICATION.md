# Verification — who can actually BUY finished leather goods from India

You want to **sell finished leather goods made in India** to these companies. So the only
relevant prospects are those that **buy in / import / distribute / stock-hold finished leather
goods for resale**. I re-checked each company and split them into three groups.

Legend used in the spreadsheet (`Leather_Goods_Wholesalers.xlsx`, column "Fit"):

- **Yes** – importer / distributor / stock-holding wholesaler that resells finished goods → **your target buyers**.
- **Maybe** – company that makes its own products or is an own-design brand → only relevant if you can offer **OEM / private-label manufacturing** (they buy *production*, not a finished catalogue). Several already produce in Asia.
- **No** – leather **materials/hides/footwear** supplier, or **artisan / own-make** producer → not a buyer of a finished-goods catalogue.

Totals: **64 companies → 30 "Yes", 18 "Maybe", 16 "No".**
See the **"Priority targets"** tab of the spreadsheet for just the 30 "Yes" companies.
